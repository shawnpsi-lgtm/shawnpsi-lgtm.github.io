How r2music works

01 · Find

Finds your music in rekordbox

The Mac app reads your rekordbox library: tracks and playlists, with their beat grids, waveforms and cues.

02 · Upload

Uploads it once, to your own bucket

Each track goes to your Cloudflare R2 bucket once, named by its SHA-256, so nothing is stored twice. Playlists sit beside them in an open lists.json.

03 · Sync

Syncs to your laptop and phone

Both open the same library, so a playlist saved on the laptop is on your phone.

04 · Play

Manage, mix and stream

  • Filessearch, sort and drag into lists, as in the demo.
  • Web DJBEAT FX loads tracks from the bucket.
  • Streamsigned URLs, straight from your bucket.
