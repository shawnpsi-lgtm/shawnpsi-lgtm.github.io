r2music

Your music. Your storage.

Role Design & Engineering
Year 2026
Tools TypeScript, Vite, Cloudflare Workers & R2, Electron

Overview

r2music is a music library that lives in your own Cloudflare R2 bucket. Sign in with GitHub, connect your bucket, drop files in, and play them anywhere: in the browser, in a Mac app, or loaded straight onto a deck in BEAT FX.

Problem

DJs and collectors keep their music in files, not streaming catalogs, and those files end up scattered:

  • Libraries are stuck on one machine. The crate lives on a laptop, so it isn't there on a phone, another computer, or a gig.
  • Cloud lockers own your files. Hosted music lockers keep your audio on their servers, under their limits and pricing, and close when the service does.
  • DJ prep doesn't travel. Beat grids, cue points and waveforms from rekordbox stay on the drive they were analysed on.

Solution

Your audio never touches r2music's servers. Each user brings their own R2 bucket, and the app is a thin layer over it:

  • Bring your own bucket. A Cloudflare Worker handles GitHub sign-in, stores each user's R2 keys encrypted, and hands the browser short-lived signed URLs. Uploads and playback go straight between the browser and the bucket.
  • An open bucket format. Tracks are stored by the SHA-256 of their contents, so duplicates upload once. A single lists.json holds the library and playlists, and writers save it conditionally and merge on conflict, so two devices can edit at once without losing changes.
  • One library, many apps. Any app that reads the bucket format can use it. BEAT FX already does: its R2 button opens a picker of your r2music library and loads the track onto the deck.

Desktop

A macOS app wraps the web app and adds what the browser can't reach: your local DJ software and drives.

  • rekordbox import. Reads the local rekordbox library, tracks and playlists, with no export step.
  • DJ USB import and export. Plugged-in CDJ drives show up in the sidebar. Drag tracks or a list onto one to export, and it's written into rekordbox's own database format, leaving existing entries alone and backing the databases up first.
  • Analysis comes along. Beat grids, waveforms and cues from rekordbox are kept in the bucket and written back to the drive on export, so tracks arrive ready to play on a CDJ.
