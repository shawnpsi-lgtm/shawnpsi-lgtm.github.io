r music

r music is live.

It puts your music in your own Cloudflare R2 bucket.

The Mac app finds your music in rekordbox.

Your tracks, your playlists, your beat grids, waveforms and cues.

Each track uploads to your bucket once.

It’s named by its SHA-256, so nothing is ever stored twice.

Your playlists sit right beside it, in an open lists.json.

Your laptop and your phone open that same bucket.

Save a playlist on your laptop. It’s already on your phone.

Songs stream straight from your bucket, over signed URLs.

Then rx3 opens it.

A Pioneer XDJ-RX3, in your browser.

Your r music library shows up as USB1.

Load a track on each deck and mix.

Echo, Reverb, Dub Echo and Space, ported from the unit’s own firmware.

It all runs on your device, so there’s no lag.

rekordbox → your bucket → your laptop and phone → rx3.

Mix anywhere.
