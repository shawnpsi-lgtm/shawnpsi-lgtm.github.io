r music is live

November 5, 2026
from Shawn

The short version

Your music in one place you own. Play it and mix it from your laptop or your phone.

r music keeps your songs and playlists in your own cloud storage, so they’re on every device you use. rx3 is a DJ setup in a browser tab that plays straight from it. Both are out today.

r music

  • Your music, your storage. Songs live in your own Cloudflare R2 bucket. Nobody else holds them.
  • Brings your rekordbox library over. The Mac app grabs your songs, playlists and cues and uploads them for you.
  • Same library everywhere. Make a playlist on your laptop and it’s on your phone at r2.shawnsingh.me.
  • No duplicates. Each song is uploaded once, even if it’s in ten playlists.

rx3

  • A real DJ unit in your browser. It’s the Pioneer XDJ-RX3: two decks, a mixer and the touch screen.
  • The real effects. Echo, reverb, dub echo, space and the rest, ported from the unit itself.
  • No lag. The sound runs on your device, not on a server.
  • Your r music library is built in. It shows up as USB1, ready to load.

Get started

  1. Make an R2 bucket in your Cloudflare account if you don’t have one yet.
  2. Open r2.shawnsingh.me, sign in with GitHub and connect your bucket.
  3. Add your music. Drop files in on the web, or get the Mac app to bring your rekordbox library.
  4. Mix it. Open rx3, pick USB1 and load a song onto each deck.
Open r music Mac app Open rx3

Good to know

  • What’s R2? Cloudflare’s file storage. You make a bucket in your own Cloudflare account, and it has a free tier to start with.
  • Do I need rekordbox? No. You can upload songs straight from the web app.
  • Which Macs? The Mac app is for Apple Silicon. It isn’t notarized yet, so the first time, unzip it into Applications, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  • Can I try rx3 without r music? Yes. Drop a song onto the page, or pick a folder on your computer as USB2. Songs you drop in stay in that tab.
  • Can I leave? Anytime. Your songs are plain files in your bucket, and your playlists are in a readable lists.json next to them.
