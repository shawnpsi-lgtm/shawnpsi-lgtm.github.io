Press release

r music is live

For immediate release
San Francisco, CA · November 5, 2026

Announcement

Your music library, in your own bucket, on every device you mix from.

SAN FRANCISCO, November 5, 2026. Shawn Singh today released r music 1.0, a music library that lives in the listener’s own Cloudflare R2 bucket rather than on a single laptop. It launches alongside rx3, a Pioneer XDJ-RX3 that runs in the browser and plays straight from that library. Together they let a DJ prepare a set in rekordbox at home and pick it up on any laptop or phone, with the same tracks, playlists and cues.

What is r music

r music is a Mac app and a web app that share one library. The Mac app reads your rekordbox collection, tracks and playlists along with their beat grids, waveforms and cues, and uploads it to a Cloudflare R2 bucket that you own.

Each track is uploaded once and named by its SHA-256, so nothing is stored twice. Playlists sit beside the music in an open lists.json file. The web app at r2.shawnsingh.me signs in with GitHub, connects to the same bucket, and streams through signed URLs that come straight from it. The files stay in your account, in formats you can read without r2music.

What is rx3

rx3 is an XDJ-RX3 for the browser: the unit’s touch screen, its buttons, two decks and the mixer. Its effects are ported from the RX3’s own firmware: Beat FX Echo, Reverb, Flanger, Phaser, Filter and Trans, and all six Sound Color FX, Space, Dub Echo, Sweep, Noise, Crush and Filter. Two more Beat FX, Drum and Noise, come from BEAT FX.

Everything runs on the device in Web Audio, so there is no server in the audio path and no added latency. On a computer, the keyboard under the screen maps every control, with each key lit by what it drives.

How it fits together

  1. Prepare in rekordbox. Build playlists and set cues on your Mac as you do today.
  2. Upload once with r music. The Mac app sends new tracks and your playlists to your R2 bucket.
  3. Open it anywhere. Your laptop and phone read the same library, so a playlist saved on one is on the other.
  4. Mix in rx3. The r music library appears as USB1 on rx3’s source screen. A folder on your computer is USB2, and you can drop in any song to play it on the spot.

Availability

r music 1.0 is available today for Apple Silicon Macs and on the web at r2.shawnsingh.me. rx3 is available at shawnsingh.me/rx3. Both run in current desktop and mobile browsers.
