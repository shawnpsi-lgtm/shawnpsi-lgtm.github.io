rx3 + r2

Access and manage your rekordbox library from anywhere. Whether you’re prepping for a set, growing your library or DJing live, rx3 + r2 brings music streaming and performance DJ software together.

Open r music Download for Mac Open rx3
  1. Find. The Mac app reads your rekordbox library: tracks and playlists, with their beat grids, waveforms and cues.
  2. Upload once. Each track goes to your Cloudflare R2 bucket once, named by its SHA-256, so nothing is stored twice. Playlists sit beside them in an open lists.json.
  3. Sync. Your laptop and phone open the same bucket, so a playlist saved on the laptop is already on your phone.
  4. Play. Sort and upload in Files, stream over signed URLs straight from your bucket, or open rx3, where your library is USB1, and mix.
