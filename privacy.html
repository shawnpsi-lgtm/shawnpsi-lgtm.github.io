Privacy

Your data

What Shawn AI keeps when you sign in,
and how to erase it.

Privacy policy

This covers Shawn AI, the chat on shawnsingh.me, and its API, run by Shawn Singh. Last updated September 27, 2026.

Without signing in

Your questions go to the Shawn AI server and on to Groq, which generates the answer. Nothing you type is saved. For each answer, the server keeps only its size in tokens, how long it took and which part of the site asked, for the public usage dashboard. Your IP address is used briefly for rate limiting and isn't stored.

When you sign in with Google or LinkedIn

Signing in is optional. It exists only to save your chats. From your Google or LinkedIn account, Shawn AI receives your name, email address and an account ID, and nothing else: no contacts, files or other account data. It stores those, plus the chats you have while signed in, in a Cloudflare D1 database.

This data is used only to show your saved chats back to you. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or to train AI models. Shawn can see the list of accounts and saved chats.

Deleting your data

In the chat, click Delete my data. That erases your account and every saved chat at once, permanently. You can also email shawn@visuallyrepresented.co to ask for deletion.

Services involved

Cloudflare hosts the server and database. Groq generates answers; questions are sent without your name or email. Google and LinkedIn handle sign-in under their own privacy policies. How it all works is written up on the API page.
