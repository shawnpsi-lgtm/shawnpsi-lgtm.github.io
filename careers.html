Shawn AI FDE Intern (Summer 27)

LocationSan Francisco, CA
TeamForward Deployed Engineering
TermSummer 2027

Shawn AI answers questions about Shawn Singh's work. It runs as the chat on shawnsingh.me, a public API and CLI, a Slack app and an MCP server, all served from one Cloudflare Worker with Groq models behind it.

We're looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer Intern to join for Summer 2027. You'll work directly with the people who use Shawn AI, figure out what they actually need, and ship it end to end: from the prompt and the data it reads, to the Worker, to the page they see.

Responsibilities

  • Take Shawn AI into new places (teams' Slack workspaces, AI tools that speak MCP, recruiters' workflows) and make it work well there.
  • Design, build and maintain features across the Worker, the D1 database and the site's front end.
  • Measure answer quality and cost with the eval suite and usage data, and improve both.
  • Turn feedback from users into small, well-scoped changes, and ship them.
  • Write clear docs for the API, the CLI and integrations.

Qualifications

  • Pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, Data Science, Cognitive Science or a related field.
  • Experience with JavaScript or TypeScript, and comfort working across front end and back end.
  • Familiarity with LLM APIs, prompting and evaluating model output.
  • Good judgment about users: you like talking to them as much as building for them.
  • Clear written and verbal communication.

Nice to have

  • Experience with Cloudflare Workers, serverless platforms or SQL databases.
  • Something you built with an LLM that other people actually use.
