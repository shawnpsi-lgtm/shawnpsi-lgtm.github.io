Shawn AI FDE Intern (Summer 27)

LocationSan Francisco, CA
TeamForward Deployed Engineering
TermSummer 2027

AI is changing what a business can build, and most of that value is still waiting to be delivered. Closing the gap takes engineers who can work at the frontier of what models can do and sit next to the people who need it working.

We're looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer Intern to join for Summer 2027. You'll be embedded with customers: learning how their business runs, finding the problems worth solving, and designing, building and deploying AI solutions that go into production and change how they work.

Responsibilities

  • Work directly with customers to understand their operations, data and goals, and turn them into clear technical plans.
  • Design, build and deploy end-to-end AI applications, from data pipelines and model integration to the interfaces people use every day.
  • Prototype quickly at the frontier of what current models can do, then harden what works for production.
  • Integrate solutions with customers' existing systems, tools and workflows.
  • Measure results against business outcomes, iterate on feedback, and bring what you learn in the field back into how we build.
  • Communicate progress, tradeoffs and results to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Qualifications

  • Pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering or a related field.
  • Strong programming skills in Python, JavaScript/TypeScript or another modern language.
  • Solid foundations in data structures, algorithms and working with data.
  • Hands-on experience building with LLMs or other machine learning tools.
  • A customer-first mindset: you like understanding the problem as much as building the solution.
  • Clear written and verbal communication, and comfort working autonomously in ambiguous situations.

Nice to have

  • Experience shipping software to real users, through an internship, a startup or a project people actually use.
  • Familiarity with cloud platforms, APIs and databases.
  • Interest in how businesses operate across industries like finance, healthcare, energy or manufacturing.
